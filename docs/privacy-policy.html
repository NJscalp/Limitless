Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

QuantView (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates LookScroll. This Privacy Policy explains how the LookScroll app and our related infrastructure handle your information when you use the app.

Face data (App Review): For Apple’s Face Data questions (Guideline 2.1), see Section 15 — Face Data Policy, the quotable summary at Section 15.6, and the TrueDepth / ARKit Q&A at Section 15.7.

1. Data we process

Depending on how you use LookScroll, we or your device may process the following categories:

  • Profile: Display name you enter to continue (stored on your device). Age and gender are optional and only used for personalization in the app if you choose to provide them.
  • Face data (AI looksmax report): Face data means the user selfie you capture or select (JPEG). To produce the structured AI report, a compressed JPEG may be sent from the app to infrastructure operated by QuantView. That infrastructure may forward the image to Anthropic (Claude) solely to generate the analysis returned to your app. Numeric scores and text recommendations from the analysis may be stored locally on your device. An optional copy of your last scan image may be stored only inside the app sandbox so you can see it in the UI. See Sections 2, 7, and 9 for details on transmission, third parties, and retention.
  • Exercise verification: Live camera frames for rep counting and task verification — processed on your device using Apple frameworks. Those frames are not uploaded for that feature (see Sections 3 and 15).
  • Screen Time / blocking: Which apps you choose to block and related selections, via Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls). Apple’s systems enforce and store the relevant selections according to iOS behavior.
  • Tasks and streaks: Exercise completion, unlock tasks, streaks, and related stats (stored locally on the device).
  • Subscriptions: Purchase and entitlement status via Apple (StoreKit) and, where applicable, RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card number from the app.
  • Support: If you email us, we receive the content you send (e.g. address, message text).

2. Face scan: consent, AI-only report, and what is not uploaded

In-app consent (required for network AI). Before your selfie is sent for the full cloud AI face report, LookScroll shows an in-app disclosure and asks you to agree. You must tap to allow that processing. If you do not agree, the app does not send your image for that cloud AI report. You may also disable the AI face scan feature later in the app’s settings, which stops using the network for that report (local geometry-only processing may still occur for basic on-device checks where applicable).

What the full report is. The structured looksmax-style face report you see after consent is produced using cloud AI (QuantView backend and Anthropic). There is no separate “device-only” version of that same full AI report; the product is designed so that the complete AI report path uses the disclosure and transmission described here.

Exercise verification is separate. Rep counting and exercise verification use the camera on-device only and do not upload your video or photos for that purpose (see Section 3).

Our servers. When you use the AI face report, our backend receives the JPEG only to perform the analysis and return results to the app. Face images are deleted from our servers after the analysis response is sent to your app; we do not retain face scan image files on our servers after each request completes. Processing may use memory only during the request. We do not sell your face images.

Anthropic. Our backend may send the image to Anthropic’s API so Claude can analyze it. Where Anthropic’s API and our agreement allow, we configure requests so that your content is processed only to return the analysis to you and is not used to train Anthropic’s models. Where the Anthropic Messages API supports it, we also configure requests so that the conversation is not stored for later retrieval (for example, using API parameters that disable storing the message, where available). Anthropic’s own handling of inputs and outputs is governed by their policies; please read Anthropic’s Privacy Policy. Commercial terms such as Zero Data Retention, if applicable, are contractual matters between QuantView and Anthropic.

If the app ever calls Anthropic directly from the device (for example in development or fallback configurations), similar non-storage settings are applied where supported. Your primary production path may use our backend.

3. Camera usage

The camera is used for: (a) optional face scans for the AI report, after you provide consent as described above; (b) TrueDepth / ARKit–based guided scanning with a real-time on-device mesh overlay (see Section 15); and (c) real-time exercise verification (on-device only, no upload of those frames for verification).

4. Local storage on your device

Scores, settings, task history, and similar data are stored on your device using Apple’s standard storage (e.g. UserDefaults and the app sandbox). A file for your last face scan image may exist only in app storage until you delete the app or clear relevant data in the app.

5. Screen Time and app blocking

LookScroll uses Apple’s Screen Time / Family Controls APIs so you can select apps to block. Apple’s systems manage authorization and enforcement according to iOS.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (StoreKit). We may use RevenueCat to manage and validate subscription status. We do not receive your payment card details through the app.

7. Third parties

  • Apple: iOS, StoreKit, Screen Time / Family Controls, and on-device frameworks (e.g. Vision, AVFoundation).
  • RevenueCat: Subscription status and related metadata, as applicable.

Anthropic’s privacy policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

8. Data sharing and sale of data

We do not sell your personal information. Face scan images are transmitted only to provide the AI analysis you initiate after consent. We do not use your face scan for advertising. Subscription-related identifiers flow through Apple (and RevenueCat as applicable) under their terms.

9. Retention

We configure requests to avoid unnecessary storage where the API allows, and so that content is not used for model training where applicable under Anthropic’s API and our agreement.

15. Face Data Policy (App Store Review — Apple Guideline 2.1)

This section answers how LookScroll collects, uses, shares, stores, and retains face-related data, including TrueDepth / ARKit face tracking and optional still-image analysis. You may quote this section in App Store Connect review notes.

15.1 What face data does the app collect?

We distinguish two categories:

  • (A) Live TrueDepth / ARKit face tracking (on-device only): When you use the guided face scan, the app uses Apple’s ARKit and the TrueDepth camera, where supported, to estimate face pose (e.g. head orientation) and to render a real-time 3D face mesh overlay for visualization and guidance. This processing runs on your device. We do not continuously upload a stream of face anchors, depth maps, or mesh vertices to our servers, and we do not use this live data to create a separate biometric identity profile outside the scan session.
  • (B) Optional still images (JPEG) for the AI looksmax report: If you complete the flow and grant the in-app consent for network AI processing, the app may capture one or more still images (typically JPEG) from the camera buffer (including frames derived from the scan) and send them to QuantView-operated infrastructure solely to generate the structured analysis shown in the app. This is optional and gated by explicit in-app consent (see Section 2).

We do not collect a separate “Face ID” template from Apple’s authentication system. We do not use ARKit to derive a persistent unique face identifier for advertising.

15.2 All planned uses of collected face data

  • Live AR overlay & scan guidance: Show the wireframe / mask and prompt you to hold frontal and angled poses so a usable still capture can be taken.
  • Optional cloud AI analysis: If you consent, still JPEG(s) are analyzed to produce numeric scores and text recommendations (the “looksmax”-style report) returned to the app.
  • On-device display & app features: Store analysis results and an optional last-scan image locally in the app sandbox so you can see your report and related UI (e.g. paywall preview) without re-capturing each launch.
  • Exercise verification (separate use): Camera video for exercise verification is processed on-device for rep/verification; we do not upload those frames for that purpose (see Section 3).

15.3 Is face data shared with third parties? Where is it stored?

  • Live ARKit / TrueDepth processing: Stays on the device; not transmitted to QuantView as a live stream.
  • Optional AI report (if you consent): JPEG data is sent over HTTPS to QuantView’s backend, which processes it and may forward the image to Anthropic (Claude API) solely to produce the analysis returned to your app. Anthropic’s handling is governed by their policies (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). We configure API requests, where supported, so content is not used to train Anthropic models and is not stored for longer than needed to return the response, as described in Section 2 and Section 9.
  • Local storage: The app may keep your last scan image file and structured scores in the iOS app sandbox on your device until you delete the app or use in-app deletion where available.

We do not sell face images. We do not use face scan images for third-party advertising.

15.4 How long is face data retained?

  • On-device: Until you uninstall the app or remove data through controls we provide, as applicable.
  • QuantView servers: Face images are used only to fulfill your analysis request; we delete face scan image files from our servers after the response is delivered, as stated in Section 9.
  • Anthropic: Retention of API inputs/outputs is governed by Anthropic’s API and privacy documentation; we apply non-training and minimal-retention settings where the API allows (see Section 2).
  • Live AR session data: Not retained as a separate log of face meshes on our servers; it exists transiently in device memory during the camera session.

15.5 Where in this privacy policy is face data explained?

Face-related processing is covered in: Section 1 (summary), Section 2 (consent, cloud AI path, Anthropic, what is not uploaded), Section 3 (camera), Section 4 (local storage), Section 7 (third parties), Section 8 (sharing), Section 9 (retention), Section 12 (your choices), and this Section 15 (consolidated Face Data Policy for App Review), including Section 15.7 (TrueDepth / ARKit Q&A for Guideline 2.1).

15.7 TrueDepth / ARKit — answers to Apple Guideline 2.1 (Information Needed)

The following statements describe how LookScroll uses Apple’s face-tracking APIs on supported hardware. They are intended for App Store Review and may be copied into Resolution Center replies.

What information does the app collect using the TrueDepth API?

On supported iPhones, the app runs ARKit ARFaceTrackingConfiguration (SceneKit ARSCNView) to obtain a live ARFaceAnchor and camera frames. From that pipeline the app derives real-time face pose (e.g. head orientation / yaw) and blend-shape coefficients where needed for on-device exercise counting, plus a rendered 3D face mesh overlay for user guidance. The app does not read Apple’s separate Face ID biometric enrollment template. The app does not open AVCaptureDevice.DeviceType.builtInTrueDepthCamera as a standalone capture device; TrueDepth is engaged indirectly through ARKit face tracking, which is Apple’s supported path for this feature class.

For what purposes is this information collected?

  • Guided face scan (onboarding / Face tab): Live mesh overlay, pose prompts, and capture timing for optional still snapshots.
  • Facial exercise verification (in-app tasks): On-device rep counting and feedback from ARFaceAnchor where supported; otherwise a Vision + wide-angle front camera fallback.

Will the data be shared with third parties? Where is it stored?

Live TrueDepth / ARKit stream: Processed on-device in real time. We do not upload a continuous stream of ARFaceAnchor geometry, depth maps, or per-frame mesh vertices to our servers.

Optional still images (JPEG) for the AI looksmax report: Only if you grant the separate in-app consent for cloud processing, the app may send one or more still JPEGs over HTTPS to infrastructure we operate so the structured report can be generated; that path may involve Anthropic (Claude) as a processor solely to return analysis to the app, as disclosed in Section 2 and Section 15.3. If you do not consent, those JPEGs are not sent for cloud AI.

Local storage: The app may store your last scan image and numeric/text results in the iOS app sandbox for display inside the app.
