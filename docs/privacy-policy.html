Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

QuantView (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates Limitless. This Privacy Policy explains how the Limitless app handles your information.

1. Data we process

Limitless may process the following:

  • Profile: Name and age you enter in the app (stored on your device only).
  • Face scan: A selfie and derived analysis scores. For the full AI report, a compressed image may be sent over the network to infrastructure we operate. Our server uses it only to return analysis to the app and does not keep your photo on disk after the request. Numeric results may be saved locally on your device. An optional copy of your last scan image may be stored only inside the app’s sandbox so you can see it in the UI.
  • Exercise verification: Live camera frames for rep counting and task verification — processed on your device only (see Section 3).
  • Screen Time / blocking: Which apps you choose to block and related settings, via Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls), under iOS control.
  • Tasks and streaks: Exercise completion, unlock tasks, and streak data (stored locally).
  • Subscriptions: Status via Apple and, where applicable, RevenueCat — not your card number.

2. On-device vs. network (face scan)

Exercise verification uses Apple frameworks (e.g. Vision, AVFoundation) on your device. Those camera frames are not uploaded for that feature.

Face scan (looksmax report): To generate the structured scores, the app may send a JPEG to our backend. Our backend may forward that image to Anthropic solely to produce the analysis returned to the app. We do not sell face data. Our server is not intended to retain face images after each analysis completes.

3. Camera usage

The camera is used for (a) optional face scans to build your report and (b) real-time exercise verification. Exercise verification does not upload your video or photos.

4. Local storage

Scores, settings, and task history are stored on your device using Apple’s standard storage (e.g. UserDefaults and the app sandbox). A last-scan image file may exist only in app storage until you delete the app or reset data in the app.

5. Screen Time and app blocking

Limitless uses Apple’s Screen Time / Family Controls to let you block apps. That information is handled by iOS.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (StoreKit). We may use RevenueCat to manage and validate subscription status. We do not receive your payment card details.

7. Third parties

We do not use advertising SDKs or third-party ad tracking. Services we use may include: Apple (StoreKit, system frameworks), RevenueCat (subscription status), and — for face scan analysis only — our backend and Anthropic as described in Section 2. See Anthropic’s privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Face scan images are transmitted only as needed to perform the analysis you request, as described above. Subscription-related data flows through Apple (and RevenueCat as applicable).

9. Retention

On your device: App data remains until you uninstall the app or use in-app reset/delete options. On our server: We do not intend to retain face scan images after each analysis response is sent to the app.

10. Children’s privacy

Limitless is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Data deletion

You can remove your data by uninstalling the app or using reset/delete options inside the app, where available.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

13. Contact
