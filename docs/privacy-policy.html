Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

QuantView (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates Limitless. This Privacy Policy explains how the Limitless app and our related infrastructure handle your information when you use the app.

Face data (App Review): For Apple Guideline 2.1, see Section 15 — Face Data Policy and the quotable text at Section 15.6.

1. Data we process

Depending on how you use Limitless, we or your device may process the following categories:

  • Profile: Display name you enter to continue (stored on your device). Age and gender are optional and only used for personalization if you provide them.
  • Face data (AI looksmax report): Face data means the user selfie you capture or select (JPEG). To produce the structured AI report, a compressed JPEG may be sent from the app to infrastructure operated by QuantView. That infrastructure may forward the image to Anthropic (Claude) solely to generate the analysis returned to your app. Numeric scores and text recommendations from the analysis may be stored locally on your device. An optional copy of your last scan image may be stored only inside the app sandbox so you can see it in the UI. See Sections 2, 7, 9, and 15 (TrueDepth/ARKit vs. cloud JPEG).
  • Exercise verification: Live camera frames for rep counting and task verification — processed on your device using Apple frameworks. Those frames are not uploaded for that feature (see Sections 3 and 15).
  • Screen Time / blocking: Which apps you choose to block and related selections, via Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls). Apple’s systems enforce and store the relevant selections according to iOS behavior.
  • Tasks and streaks: Exercise completion, unlock tasks, streaks, and related stats (stored locally on the device).
  • Subscriptions: Purchase and entitlement status via Apple (StoreKit) and, where applicable, RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card number from the app.
  • Support: If you email us, we receive the content you send (e.g. address, message text).

2. Face scan: consent, AI-only report, and what is not uploaded

In-app consent (required for network AI). Before your selfie is sent for the full cloud AI face report, Limitless shows an in-app disclosure and asks you to agree. You must tap to allow that processing. If you do not agree, the app does not send your image for that cloud AI report. You may also disable the AI face scan feature later in the app’s settings, which stops using the network for that report (local geometry-only processing may still occur for basic on-device checks where applicable).

What the full report is. The structured looksmax-style face report you see after consent is produced using cloud AI (QuantView backend and Anthropic). There is no separate “device-only” version of that same full AI report; the product is designed so that the complete AI report path uses the disclosure and transmission described here.

Exercise verification is separate. Rep counting and exercise verification use the camera on-device only and do not upload your video or photos for that purpose (see Section 3).

Our servers. When you use the AI face report, our backend receives the JPEG only to perform the analysis and return results to the app. Face images are deleted from our servers after the analysis response is sent to your app; we do not retain face scan image files on our servers after each request completes. Processing may use memory only during the request. We do not sell your face images.

Anthropic. Our backend may send the image to Anthropic’s API so Claude can analyze it. Where Anthropic’s API and our agreement allow, we configure requests so that your content is processed only to return the analysis to you and is not used to train Anthropic’s models. Where the Anthropic Messages API supports it, we also configure requests so that the conversation is not stored for later retrieval (for example, using API parameters that disable storing the message, where available). Anthropic’s own handling of inputs and outputs is governed by their policies; please read Anthropic’s Privacy Policy. Commercial terms such as Zero Data Retention, if applicable, are contractual matters between QuantView and Anthropic.

If the app ever calls Anthropic directly from the device (for example in development or fallback configurations), similar non-storage settings are applied where supported. Your primary production path may use our backend.

3. Camera usage

The camera is used for: (a) optional face scans for the AI report, after you provide consent as described above; (b) TrueDepth / ARKit–based guided scanning with a real-time on-device face mesh overlay and pose (see Section 15); and (c) real-time exercise verification (on-device only, no upload of those frames for verification).

4. Local storage on your device

Scores, settings, task history, and similar data are stored on your device using Apple’s standard storage (e.g. UserDefaults and the app sandbox). A file for your last face scan image may exist only in app storage until you delete the app or clear relevant data in the app.

5. Screen Time and app blocking

Limitless uses Apple’s Screen Time / Family Controls APIs so you can select apps to block. Apple’s systems manage authorization and enforcement according to iOS.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (StoreKit). We may use RevenueCat to manage and validate subscription status. We do not receive your payment card details through the app.

7. Third parties

We do not use advertising SDKs or third-party ad tracking in the app for profiling. Services that may receive or process data in connection with Limitless include:

  • Apple: iOS, StoreKit, Screen Time / Family Controls, and on-device frameworks (e.g. Vision, AVFoundation, ARKit).
  • RevenueCat: Subscription status and related metadata, as applicable.
  • QuantView infrastructure: Backend services that receive face scan images only to perform the analysis you request.
  • Anthropic: Cloud AI analysis of face data (user selfie) as described in Section 2; we configure the API so content is not used for model training where applicable under Anthropic’s API and our agreement.

Anthropic’s privacy policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

8. Data sharing and sale of data

We do not sell your personal information. Face scan images are transmitted only to provide the AI analysis you initiate after consent. We do not use your face scan for advertising. Subscription-related identifiers flow through Apple (and RevenueCat as applicable) under their terms.

9. Retention

On your device: App data remains until you uninstall the app or use reset/delete options inside the app, where available.

On our servers: Face images are deleted from our servers after the analysis response is returned to your app. We do not keep face scan image files on our servers after each request completes.

Anthropic: Retention and use of content sent to Anthropic’s API are described in Anthropic’s documentation and privacy policy. We configure requests to avoid unnecessary storage where the API allows, and so that content is not used for model training where applicable under Anthropic’s API and our agreement.

10. Security

We use HTTPS between the app and our backend where applicable. You should keep your device secure and use the latest iOS version supported by the app.

11. Children’s privacy

Limitless is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Your choices and data deletion

You can stop using the AI face report by declining consent when prompted or by turning off the related option in Settings (where available). You can remove data stored on your device by uninstalling the app or using reset/delete options inside the app, where available.

13. International users

If you use the app from outside the country where our servers are located, your information may be processed in those locations. By using the service, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred as needed to provide the features you choose.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after changes may mean you accept the updated policy; if required by law, we will seek additional consent.

15. Face Data Policy (Apple Guideline 2.1)

This section describes collection, use, sharing, storage, and retention of face-related data. You may reference it in App Store Connect.

15.1 What face data does the app collect?

  • (A) Live TrueDepth / ARKit (on-device): Face pose and a real-time 3D mesh overlay for guided scanning. This runs on your device. We do not upload a continuous stream of face anchors, depth data, or mesh vertices to our servers.
  • (B) Optional JPEG(s) for AI report: If you grant in-app consent, still images may be sent to QuantView infrastructure and to Anthropic as in Section 2.

We do not use Apple’s Face ID authentication templates. We do not use ARKit to build a persistent face ID for ads.

15.2 Planned uses

  • AR overlay and scan guidance on device.
  • Optional cloud AI analysis after consent.
  • Local storage of scores and last scan image in the app sandbox.
  • Exercise verification: camera on-device only; no upload for that feature (Section 3).

15.3 Third parties and where data is stored

  • Live ARKit/TrueDepth: remains on device.
  • With consent: HTTPS to QuantView backend; may forward JPEG to Anthropic. See Section 7 and Anthropic’s policy.
  • Local: app sandbox on your iPhone.

15.4 Retention

  • Device: until uninstall or in-app deletion where available.
  • Our servers: face image files deleted after the response is sent (Section 9).
  • Anthropic: per their API and privacy policy; we apply minimal retention / no training where supported (Section 2).
  • Live AR session: transient device memory during the session; not logged as mesh archives on our servers.

15.5 Where else this is covered

Sections 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 12, and this 15.

15.6 Quotable text (for Apple Review)

Face Data (Limitless). The app uses the TrueDepth camera and ARKit on the device to show a real-time face mesh overlay and head pose for guided face scanning. That live face-tracking data is not uploaded to our servers as a stream and is not sold. If you opt in, the app may send one or more still face images (JPEG) over HTTPS to QuantView’s backend for an optional AI looksmax report; our backend may forward those images to Anthropic (Claude) only to generate the analysis returned to the app. Without your in-app consent, those images are not sent for cloud AI. Exercise verification uses the camera on-device only and does not upload those frames for verification. The app may store your last scan image and scores locally in the app sandbox. Server-side face images are deleted after the analysis response is sent, as described in the full Privacy Policy.
